Recipe Radar Forever lists every profession recipe sold by a vendor in WoW Forever. For each recipe it shows who sells it, where the vendor stands, what it costs, and which of your characters can learn it. Use it to find the vendor recipes you are still missing, without checking every vendor by hand.

## What it does

- Covers all **768 vendor recipes** for Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Cooking, Enchanting, Engineering, First Aid, Fishing, Leatherworking and Tailoring, including the WoW Forever recipes: **Merchant's Favor** recipes and certifications from the trade camps, the Skyforged quartermasters, and the vendors in Mount Hyjal, Riverglades and Zephras Isle.
- Shows costs in gold, Merchant's Favor or token items.
- **Radar tab:** every recipe vendor in a zone, with a zone map.
- **Recipes tab:** every vendor recipe of one profession and all the places that sell it.
- Search by recipe, vendor or zone name.
- Filters by faction, profession and availability: learnable now, learnable later, or already known, for every character on your realm.
- Shift-click a vendor to mark it on the world map and minimap. Uses TomTom if it is installed, otherwise the game's own map pin and waypoint.
- Works on its own. If QuestieDB is installed, it also picks up newer vendor data from it automatically.

## Commands

| Command | Action |
|---|---|
| `/rrf` | Open or close the window (also from the minimap button, the AddOn compartment or a key binding) |
| `/rrf options` | Options |
| `/rrf report` | Report a bug or wrong vendor data |

## Credits

Recipe Radar Forever is a port of the 2006 addon **Recipe Radar** by **Jay Miller**, rewritten for the WoW Forever addon API with a new recipe database.

Changes from the original:

- WoW Forever recipes and vendors, with Merchant's Favor costs
- Zone maps drawn from the game's own map art
- Search box
- QuestieDB import
- Modern options and map pin system
