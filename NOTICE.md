## Vendor data

`RegionData.lua` and `RecipeData.lua` are generated by `tools/build_data.py` from:

- **QuestieDB** (https://github.com/Questie/QuestieDB), GPLv3: vendor NPCs, factions
  and spawn coordinates for WoW Forever. QuestieDB's data was imported from Questie
  (https://github.com/Questie/Questie); see QuestieDB's LICENSE.md for its history.
- **AllTheThings** WoW Forever data: required skill, Merchant's Favor and token
  costs, reputation requirements and a few vendor recipes not in QuestieDB.
- **ForeverChanges** (https://foreverchanges.pro): used to cross-check recipe counts.

At runtime the addon also reads an installed QuestieDB (if present) to add
vendor data newer than the shipped snapshot. That data is not saved.
