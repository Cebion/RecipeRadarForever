# Recipe Radar Forever

## 2.2.0

- Search box: filter both tabs by recipe, vendor or zone name.
- Options: delete the stored data of one of your characters.
- "Report a problem" window (`/rrf report`, options, right-click a vendor) with a
  GitHub issue link and a details block to copy.
- The minimap button can be dragged around the minimap.
- Renamed to Recipe Radar Forever: folder `RecipeRadarForever`, slash command `/rrf`.
- Fixed: options window error at login, cramped search hint and title bar buttons.

## 2.1.0

- All 768 WoW Forever vendor recipes, including 462 Forever-new recipes and the
  320 Merchant's Favor recipes and certifications, with their costs.
- Optional live import of newer vendor data from QuestieDB.
- Required skill and prices fill in from item tooltips and merchant visits.

## 2.0.0

- First release for WoW Forever (client 1.60.x): port of Jay Miller's Recipe Radar
  to the modern addon API, with zone maps from the game's map art and TomTom or
  world map pins.
